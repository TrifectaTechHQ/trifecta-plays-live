Confidence Score (0–100)

A composite statistical score reflecting how strongly the data supports a given prop. It is not a win probability — it measures the clarity of the statistical edge.

💎 85–100Strong — Clearest statistical edge. Multiple factors aligned.
🔥 70–84Good — Solid angle supported by most data points.
⚠️ 50–69Marginal — Some edge detected, but mixed signals. Proceed with caution.
🔴 0–49Skip — No clear statistical edge. Data does not favour this prop.

Early-season scores use tiered regression (career splits → career avg → league avg) to stabilize small samples.

Lean

The directional recommendation based on the confidence score:

OverData favours the Over on this prop (e.g. more Ks, more Hits, more total bases).
UnderData favours the Under on this prop.
No LeanConfidence below 60 — not enough edge to recommend a direction.

For F5 (First 5 Innings), the lean is on the total runs line (e.g. "Over 4.5" or "Under 4.5").

Wind Impact
Blowing Out Favours hitters. Wind carries the ball toward the outfield, increasing fly ball distance. HRs and total bases are more likely. Look for value on Over HR/TB props.
Blowing In Favours pitchers. Wind pushes fly balls back toward the infield, suppressing HR distance. Consider Under on HR/TB props and Over on K props.
Neutral Wind is crosswind, light (<3 mph), or the stadium has a closed roof. No significant wind effect on ball flight.

Wind impact is calculated using each park's center field compass bearing vs. the meteorological wind direction.

Prop Types Explained
K PropsPitcher strikeout line. "Over 6" = pitcher records 6+ Ks. Line is based on K/9 pace over the pitcher's actual avg IP per start (dynamic). Every pitcher gets an Over — low confidence signals a potential Under.
Hit PropsBatter hits in a game. "Over 1 Hits" = 1+ hit, "Over 2 Hits" = 2+ hits (elite hitters only). Line and confidence are dynamic per batter using their actual AB/game, batting average, and pitcher weakness. Handedness matchup applied.
TB PropsTotal bases in a game. "Over 2 TB" = 2+ total bases. Confidence reflects likelihood of hitting that specific threshold. Handedness matchup applied.
HR PropsDedicated HR probability tab. Only batters with real HR potential appear. Strong Chance = 4 power anchors (barrel% ≥12, ISO ≥.220, xSLG ≥.500, launch angle ≥15°) + elite HR score + pitcher is vulnerable. Slight Chance = 2+ anchors + solid HR score. Probability formula: 2% + (16% x HR score). Weights: barrel% (28%), ISO (20%), pitcher vulnerability (18%), xSLG (16%), launch angle (6%), hard hit% (6%), park (4%), wind (2%).
