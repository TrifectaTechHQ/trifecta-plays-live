Wind Impact
Blowing Out Favours hitters. Wind carries the ball toward the outfield, increasing fly ball distance. HRs and total bases are more likely. Look for value on Over HR/TB props.
Blowing In Favours pitchers. Wind pushes fly balls back toward the infield, suppressing HR distance. Consider Under on HR/TB props and Over on K props.
Neutral Wind is crosswind, light (<3 mph), or the stadium has a closed roof. No significant wind effect on ball flight.

Wind impact is calculated using each park's center field compass bearing vs. the meteorological wind direction.
